Climbing Expeditions in Nepal

Guided peak climbs for beginners through to experienced mountaineers

Island Peak (Imja Tse) Climbing

18 Days · Khumbu Region · Climbing Grade: Alpine PD

Summit altitude: 6,189m Pawan Bastola

One of Nepal's most popular climbing peaks, combining the classic Everest Base Camp trek with a glacier and fixed-rope summit climb. A great introduction to high-altitude mountaineering, with views of Everest, Lhotse and Makalu from the summit. Certified climbing guides and full gear support included.

Mera Peak Climbing

17 Days · Hinku Valley, Khumbu Region · Climbing Grade: Alpine F

Summit altitude: 6,476m Pawan Bastola

Nepal's highest trekking peak, offering one of the widest mountain panoramas in the world, including five of the planet's eight-thousanders. A non-technical but high-altitude climb through remote Hinku valley forests and glaciers.

Lobuche Peak Climbing

19 Days · Khumbu Region · Climbing Grade: Alpine PD+

Summit altitude: 6,119m Pawan Bastola

A technically demanding climb with a steep, exposed summit ridge, rewarding climbers with one of the closest views of Everest available from any trekking peak. Recommended for those with prior climbing or mountaineering experience.

Ama Dablam Expedition

29 Days · Khumbu Region · Climbing Grade: Technical (fixed ropes, ice & rock)

Summit altitude: 6,812m Pawan Bastola

Widely regarded as one of the most beautiful mountains in the world, Ama Dablam is a serious technical expedition requiring prior high-altitude and rope-climbing experience. Climb Nepal arranges permits, experienced climbing Sherpas, base camp logistics and full expedition support.
