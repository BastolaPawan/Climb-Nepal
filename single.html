Everest Base Camp Trek

14-day Himalayan journey through the Khumbu Valley

May 1, 2026 Pawan Bastola

The Everest Base Camp trek is Nepal's most iconic Himalayan route, blending dramatic mountain scenery, warm Sherpa hospitality and one of the greatest alpine views in the world. From the moment you leave Kathmandu, the trail gradually climbs through rhododendron forests, stone villages and glacier-fed valleys until you reach the giant amphitheatre of Everest itself.

At Climb Nepal, we organize the full experience: permits, experienced local guides, porter support, lodge reservations and a carefully paced schedule for proper acclimatization. This route is suitable for active travelers with a steady fitness level and a desire to experience high-altitude adventure without technical climbing.

Trip Highlights

  • Duration: 14 days
  • Difficulty: Moderate to challenging
  • Max Altitude: 5,364m at Kala Patthar
  • Best Time: March-May and September-November
  • Region: Khumbu, Eastern Nepal

Suggested Itinerary

  1. Day 1: Arrival in Kathmandu and briefing with your guide.
  2. Day 2: Flight to Lukla, trek to Phakding, overnight in a mountain lodge.
  3. Day 3: Phakding to Namche Bazaar, with a short acclimatization approach.
  4. Day 4: Namche rest day with optional Everest View Hotel hike.
  5. Day 5: Namche to Tengboche, passing forest trails and monastery viewpoints.
  6. Day 6: Tengboche to Dingboche, gradual alpine ascent and acclimatization.
  7. Day 7: Dingboche acclimatization hike with panoramic Khumbu views.
  8. Day 8: Dingboche to Lobuche, entering the high Himalayan zone.
  9. Day 9: Lobuche to Gorak Shep, then hike to Everest Base Camp.
  10. Day 10: Early climb to Kala Patthar for sunrise over Everest, return to Lobuche.
  11. Day 11: Lobuche to Tengboche, retracing the trail downhill.
  12. Day 12: Tengboche to Namche Bazaar for a comfortable descent day.
  13. Day 13: Namche to Lukla, farewell trek with final mountain views.
  14. Day 14: Fly back to Kathmandu and departure transfer.
