Everest Base Camp Trek: The Complete Guide

Everything you need to know before trekking to the base of the world's tallest mountain

May 1, 2026 Pawan Bastola

The Everest Base Camp trek remains Nepal's most iconic journey, taking trekkers through Sherpa villages, suspension bridges and high alpine passes before reaching the foot of Mount Everest. Our team at Climb Nepal arranges permits, guides, porters and lodge bookings so you can focus on the experience. Best seasons are spring (March-May) and autumn (September-November).

Annapurna Circuit: A Trek Through Every Landscape

From subtropical valleys to the high Thorong La pass

April 20, 2026 Pawan Bastola

The Annapurna Circuit takes you through some of the most diverse scenery in Nepal, from terraced rice fields to the Tibetan plateau-like Manang valley, crossing the Thorong La pass at 5,416m. Climb Nepal offers fully guided packages with experienced local guides.

Island Peak Climbing: A Beginner's Gateway to Himalayan Summits

A practical introduction to high-altitude climbing in the Khumbu region

April 5, 2026 Pawan Bastola

Island Peak (Imja Tse), standing at 6,189m, is one of Nepal's most popular climbing peaks for trekkers wanting their first taste of technical mountaineering. The climb combines glacier travel, fixed ropes and a stunning summit view of Everest, Lhotse and Makalu. Climb Nepal provides certified climbing guides, gear and acclimatization support throughout the trip.
