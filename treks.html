Trekking Packages in Nepal

Hand-picked routes for every level, fully guided and supported by Climb Nepal

Everest Base Camp Trek

14 Days · Khumbu Region · Moderate to Challenging

Max altitude: 5,364m (Kala Patthar) Pawan Bastola

Walk through Sherpa villages, monasteries and high suspension bridges en route to the foot of the world's tallest mountain. Includes Kala Patthar viewpoint for the classic Everest panorama. Permits, guide, porter and tea-house accommodation all arranged by Climb Nepal.

Annapurna Circuit Trek

16 Days · Annapurna Region · Moderate to Challenging

Max altitude: 5,416m (Thorong La Pass) Pawan Bastola

One of the most diverse treks in the world, crossing subtropical valleys, Tibetan-style villages and the high Thorong La pass before descending to the Muktinath temple. A classic full-circuit experience.

Annapurna Base Camp Trek

10 Days · Annapurna Region · Moderate

Max altitude: 4,130m Pawan Bastola

A shorter, scenic alternative to the full circuit, trekking through rhododendron forests and Gurung villages up to the natural amphitheatre of Annapurna Base Camp surrounded by towering peaks.

Langtang Valley Trek

7 Days · Langtang Region · Easy to Moderate

Max altitude: 3,870m (Kyanjin Ri) Pawan Bastola

Known as "the valley of glaciers," Langtang is the closest major trekking region to Kathmandu, offering dramatic mountain scenery, Tamang culture and yak pastures, all within a short drive of the capital.

Manaslu Circuit Trek

15 Days · Manaslu Region · Challenging

Max altitude: 5,160m (Larkya La Pass) Pawan Bastola

A restricted-area trek around the world's eighth highest mountain, far quieter than Everest or Annapurna, crossing remote villages and the dramatic Larkya La pass. Special permits required, arranged by Climb Nepal.
